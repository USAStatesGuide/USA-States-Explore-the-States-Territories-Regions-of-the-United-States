Report an Error

If you notice incorrect, outdated, incomplete, or unclear information on USA Geography, we appreciate your help in identifying it.

What Can You Report?

You can report factual errors, incorrect geographical information, outdated details, broken links, missing information, or other issues that may affect the accuracy or usability of the website.

Submit a Report

You can use the form below to report an error directly to us. You do not need to provide your name or email address.

Your email address is optional. Leave it blank if you wish to remain anonymous.

Privacy: You can submit a report without providing your name or email address. If you provide an email address, it will only be used to respond to your report.

How to Report an Error

Please provide as much specific information as possible, including the name of the state, territory, island, or page where the issue appears.
